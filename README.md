# RiderVoice

Smart hands-free motorcycle rider voice intercom, helmet walkie-talkie, and cockpit voice companion.

## Features

- **Tactical Mesh Intercom**: Multi-channel encrypted motorcycle intercom with Push-To-Talk (PTT) and VOX (Voice-Activated Transmission) modes.
- **Cockpit Dashboard**: High-contrast, sunlight-readable digital speedometer, lean angle horizon indicator, compass heading, and GPS telemetry.
- **Hands-Free Voice AI Copilot**: Voice command queries ("Check speed", "Where is the nearest gas?", "Report hazard", "Mute squad") with direct audio readouts into helmet speakers.
- **Tactical Squad Radar & Hazards**: Real-time 360° proximity tracking for squad riders and community road hazard warnings (potholes, radar traps, debris, oil spills).
- **Helmet Audio & DSP**: Paired headset management (Cardo, Sena, FreedConn, Lexin), adaptive wind noise reduction DSP filter, and 3-band audio volume mixer.
- **Crash Detection & Emergency SOS**: Instant impact distress alarm with 10-second countdown and automatic coordinate broadcasting.

## Build

Built with Android Kotlin & Jetpack Compose, compiled via Gradle 9.3.1 with Android SDK 36 and Android Gradle Plugin 9.1.1.
